# Creative

## Concepts, not variations

Meta's delivery system groups ads that look and say the same thing. A new headline or colour on the same image is the same ad. A different concept changes the hook, the person, the format or the problem it speaks to. Guidance here comes from agency write-ups, not Meta rules; test in your own account.

- Aim for 8 to 12 distinct concepts a month under about $10K of monthly spend. More spend supports more.
- Each ad needs enough spend to produce one or two sales before you judge it. Ten new ads a day on a small budget starves all of them.
- 2 to 3 variations per concept is plenty.

## A ten-concept slate

1. Price offer on a product photo
2. Subscription or best-value offer
3. Tier ladder: more units, lower price each
4. Real customer review with a real photo
5. Founder letter, text first
6. How to use it
7. Product range carousel
8. The math: regular price against sale price per unit
9. Founder on camera, phone video
10. Customer or creator video run as a partnership ad

Offer ads (1 to 3) are quick to make and count as one concept between them.

## Making images

- Use real product photos as the reference. Generated packaging drifts: check every label at full size for spelling, size, flavor or variant name and net weight.
- Make one sample, get approval, then make the set. Approval of a style is not approval to launch.
- Put only the exact approved copy in the image. No dates in the image; put the deadline in the ad text or a band you can change.
- Feed 4:5 and story 9:16. Leave the top 12% and bottom 15% of a story free of text.
- State billing terms on any subscription creative: first price, renewal price, interval.
- When a deadline moves, patch or regenerate every image that mentions it before it ships anywhere.

## Reviews and claims

Quote real reviews only, word for word, from the store's review app. No health or medical claims. Customer counts and review counts need a source the owner can show.
